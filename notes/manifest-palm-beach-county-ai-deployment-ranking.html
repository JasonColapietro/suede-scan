How Suede AI SEO ranked #1 among Palm Beach County companies on The Manifest, and what it means

By Jason Colapietro, Founder and CEO of Suede AI · November 14, 2026

On October 31, 2026, Suede AI SEO ranked #1 among Palm Beach County-based companies listed in The Manifest's Florida AI Deployment directory, and in the top 25 statewide. A sentence like that is easy to put on a homepage and hard for a buyer to interpret. This note, written by Jason Colapietro, explains what the listing is, how to check it in two minutes, and how a business owner in West Palm Beach or Jupiter should weigh any agency ranking, including this one.

What the ranking says, exactly

Three details make the claim a fact instead of a slogan. The date is October 31, 2026. The category is AI Deployment, part of The Manifest's artificial intelligence consulting listings. The geography is Florida: Suede AI SEO was the highest-placed company based in Palm Beach County and sat inside the top 25 for the whole state. The live directory is at The Manifest's Florida AI Deployment page, and Suede AI keeps a dated copy of the result on its recognition page.

Suede AI repeats all three details every time the ranking comes up. Directory positions move. A ranking without its date, category and region drifts into a claim nobody can check, and an unverifiable claim is exactly what answer engines learn to discount.

How to verify it yourself

Open the directory page and note the location listed for each company. Compare the Palm Beach County entries against the recognition record. Then open Suede AI's evidence page, which holds a receipt for each public claim the company makes. If a position has changed since October 31, 2026, the dated record still tells you what was true that day, and the live page tells you what is true now. Both are useful; only a date makes them comparable.

Why a readiness tool cares about a directory listing

This site exists to score how well a website hands evidence to answer engines, so it is fair to ask why a directory listing belongs here. The answer is entity evidence. When ChatGPT or Perplexity decides whether a business is what its website says, it looks for agreement across independent sources: the site's own JSON-LD, its Google Business Profile, its LinkedIn page, press coverage and directory profiles like The Manifest's.

The Suede structured data checks reward a sameAs list because those links let an engine connect the claims. A directory profile only helps if it matches. The company name, website URL and city on the listing should read exactly as they do in your schema. For a Jupiter or Palm Beach Gardens business, a profile that lists an old domain or a different spelling is a missed corroboration, not a neutral one.

What the ranking does not mean

It is one directory's ordering, in one category and one state, on one date. It is not an industry award, a client result or a reason to skip checking the work. Jason Colapietro would rather a buyer judge Suede by receipts, and there are several. Jason Colapietro's engineering record is public: 54 pull requests merged across 46 open-source repositories he does not maintain, including Jest, Backstage and the Polkadot SDK, and nine iOS apps shipped under his own Apple developer account. Press that published the work includes Business Insider, which covered the launch of the company's SEO cohort on September 16, 2026, TechBullion and Programming Insider. When Google's Web Guide answered a search for "Suede labs ai review," its AI results layer described Suede Labs AI as "highly rated by users, earning a 5/5 rating from verified reviews" (capture). That is Google's wording, recorded as captured, not ours.

How to weigh rankings when hiring in Palm Beach County

  • Ask for the date, category and region. If an agency cannot name all three, treat the ranking as marketing copy.
  • Ask for the link. A real listing has a public URL you can open today.
  • Audit the agency's own site. Run its homepage through Suede Audit. An SEO firm whose own robots.txt blocks search crawlers is telling you something.
  • Ask what they will measure. Readiness, rankings and AI citations are different numbers. A good agency says which one it is moving and how you will see it.

Jason Colapietro built Suede AI around that last point: separate what can be checked from what has to be earned, and show proof for both.
