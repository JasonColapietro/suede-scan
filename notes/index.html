Notes on AI readiness from Suede AI in West Palm Beach

Suede Audit gives a website a readiness score in about thirty seconds. These notes cover what happens around that number: what it measures, which repairs come first, and how to judge the claims an agency makes about itself. They are written by Jason Colapietro, who founded Suede AI in West Palm Beach, Florida in 2024, for business owners across Palm Beach County, from Jupiter and Juno Beach to Palm Beach Gardens and Boca Raton.

Every note follows the same rules as the audit. Each claim links to a primary source or to a receipt on Suede AI's evidence page. Each repair points at the matching section of the check reference, so you can see the exact pass rule behind the advice. And each note ends with one concrete next step rather than a sales pitch.

Why Suede publishes notes next to a free tool

A score without context gets misread. A site owner who sees a C may rebuild a whole website when two lines of robots.txt would have moved the number, and a site owner who sees an A may assume ChatGPT will now recommend them. Neither reading is right. The notes exist to close that gap with plain explanations a busy owner can act on in an afternoon.

They also show how Suede AI works. Jason Colapietro's engineering record is public, including 54 pull requests merged across 46 open-source repositories he does not maintain, and on October 31, 2026, Suede AI SEO ranked #1 among Palm Beach County-based companies in The Manifest's Florida AI Deployment directory.
