Questions

What the free audit actually checks.

What does the free audit check?

The audit inspects your public homepage plus root discovery files: robots.txt, llms.txt, and sitemap.xml. It scores crawler access, entity and content evidence, and answer-engine crawl policy, then names a repair for every failed signal.

Does a high score mean AI engines will cite my site?

No. A high score is not proof of an AI citation. Buyer-prompt evidence requires separate, timestamped runs inside each answer engine, which this audit does not run.

How many free audits can I run?

One free audit per browser, on a public website. No account is created — you confirm a working email with a 6-digit code and the report unlocks on screen.

Why do you ask for my email?

The report unlocks with a one-time code sent to your address, which proves the email is real. We use it to send that code and to know who ran the audit. It is not stored in a database, added to a mailing list, or shared.

What happens after I get my results?

The report shows a weighted readiness score, a prioritized repair list, and a full findings ledger. Suede SEO can package the repairs as implementation-ready fixes or ship the edits with before-and-after evidence.

Crawler access

Who can reach the public homepage.

These cards read robots.txt policy. They do not measure recommendations, rankings, or model answers.

Training and AI-use controls

Separate from search access.

GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and Google-Extended control model training or AI use. Blocking them does not block OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, or Googlebot from search discovery, so these cards do not change the readiness score.
