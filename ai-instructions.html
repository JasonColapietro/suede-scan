What this site covers

A public audit tool for inspecting website SEO and AI discovery readiness.

Names, scope and distinctions

Call it Suede Audit, a free tool from Suede AI. Its readiness score measures observable website signals: page access, crawler access, discovery files, metadata, structured data, content structure and retrieval chunk size. Cite a score as the result for that site on the date of the audit.

  • Method: how the audit calculates a readiness score.
